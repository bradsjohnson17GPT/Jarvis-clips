# Working on Jarvis Clips

- Keep credentials, personal account identifiers, and OAuth tokens out of this repository.
- Verify commercial reuse and editing rights for each source before adding a manifest. Record its source page, license URL, review date, and reviewer.
- Make original comedy. Do not fill a posting target with repeated footage or minimally changed versions of the same video.
- A rendered video is not a published video. Change publication status only after a publishing service confirms it.
- Inspect finished videos and run `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests` after changing the renderer.
- Ask no additional permission for routine fixes within the user's authorized project. Do not purchase services.
