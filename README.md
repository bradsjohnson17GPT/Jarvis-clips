## Production workflow

1. Research view evidence and inspect references honestly: actual video/audio analysis, sampled frames, transcript or metadata are different evidence.
2. Clear each visual, speech, music and model source for the actual use. Keep original commentary and authentic attribution; no cloned celebrity speech or fabricated quotations.
3. Build a storyboard with a concrete hook, action and payoff. Use continuous natural narration or independently reviewed authentic recording, with speech-timed captions and music below the voice.
4. Render or edit through available tools. Descript is connected for source assembly and editing. Higgsfield still has no callable tools at the latest check. No purchases, paid subscriptions or top-ups.
5. Inspect the real final portrait framing, cuts, caption intervals, soundtrack transcript, stereo AAC, full decode and encoded loudness/headroom. Resolution and tests do not prove editorial quality.
6. Upload only an approved, inspected final file through a working connected route, verifying exact imported bytes where supported. Refresh connection and real queue under the production lock. Follow the direct connector's exact-draft approval workflow. A scheduled post is pending until the service confirms publication and returns a public URL.

[Quality tools and reproduction](docs/quality-production.md) use `requirements-quality.txt`, `config/natural-voice.json`, `scripts/story_audio.py` and `scripts/finish_story.py`. The latter still includes Downward Beacon's specific sound cues; adapt relevant effects for a different story before use. Reviewed music and required descriptions are recorded in `config/licensed-music.json`. Native sampling, output resolution and any interpolation/upscaling limitations belong in each video's own QA record; there is no blanket native-resolution claim.

The old `scripts/render.py` / Piper examples and manifests are archived for reproduction only and are guarded during the quality reset. Do not use them to refill the rejected batch. Midnight Bell remains preparation until a real finished source-cleared animation is rendered and inspected.
