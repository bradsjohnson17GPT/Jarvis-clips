# Midnight Bell original scene

The station, midnight clock, bell, original tool-cart robot, wrench, charger/cable, lights and cameras are authored in `scene.py`. Only the project-owned Downward Beacon courier hierarchy is imported; old story motion and all other loaded objects are removed. No external character/model/footage is used.

The existing eight samples and staged corrections were actually inspected. The original loose socket/cable appeared before the robot; the charger connection was hidden by the forearm. The accepted corrected investigation and before/connected/recovered close-ups are recorded by hash and exact source revision in [staging-review.json](staging-review.json). Four-sample native stills do not establish finished temporal or audiovisual quality.

A single pinned 678-frame native animation is rendering at 720×1280, 24fps and 24 samples, with no resizing or optical interpolation. It is preparation, with no finished movie or posting draft.
