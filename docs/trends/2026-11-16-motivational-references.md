## Famous-person source review

The historical test uses NASA's identified footage of Neil Armstrong descending to the Moon, with newly written historical commentary and an independent narrator. The source is https://www.nasa.gov/history/apollo-11-hd-videos/ and the exact asset is https://images.nasa.gov/details/One_Small_Step_Comparison_720p . Its context and date are supported by NASA. The original recording's audio is removed.

NASA's media policy permits factual, nonpromotional editorial use within published works and requires acknowledgment. NASA/JSC guidance distinguishes historical communication from advertising or personality endorsement; it also warns that third-party material and personality rights can require separate clearance. This specific edit reports a historical event with a motivational reflection, credits NASA, and makes no claim of NASA or astronaut endorsement. This review does not clear celebrity interviews, films, social posts, NASA merchandise or promotional uses generally. Review each actual source and use independently.
