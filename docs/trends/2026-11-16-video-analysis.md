# Current Shorts: observed comedy structure

Research date: November 16, 2026. These two public Shorts were analyzed through vidIQ's completed short-form video analysis jobs. This describes reference structure, not publishing rights or proof that the format will work for this channel. References and their soundtracks must not be republished.

## https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nkUp4V_ewDM

Here is a structural breakdown of the video:

### 1. Opening Hook & Visual Character Setup
* **Opening Hook (0:00–0:03):** Immediate introduction of characters labeled via on-screen text and physical props:
  * A **"Student"** sitting cross-armed looking disengaged.
  * A person with a printed logo over their face labeled **"ChatGPT"** acting as a tool/proxy.
  * A **"Teacher"** gesturing instructions.
* **Format:** Fast camera pans (whip-pans/quick cuts) between the three participants around a single table establish the spatial relationship and roleplay premise within 3 seconds.

---

### 2. Concrete Visual Action & Routine
* **Step 1 (Question Formulation):** The Teacher passes paper to "ChatGPT" and prompts an action. "ChatGPT" writes the math equation (`2 + 2 =`).
* **Step 2 (The Student's Struggle):** The paper is handed to the Student, who scratches her head in confusion and cannot answer.
* **Step 3 (Outsourcing to AI):** The Student immediately slides the paper over to "ChatGPT", who rapidly solves the equation (`= 4`).
* **Step 4 (Grading):** The paper goes back to the Teacher, who asks "ChatGPT" to evaluate it. "ChatGPT" gives a checkmark and writes `A+`.
* **Step 5 (Payoff/Reaction):** The Student celebrates the top grade despite having done zero actual work.

---

### 3. Shot-Change Timing & Pacing
* **Pacing:** Rapid, kinetic cuts and snaps (averaging 1–2 seconds per shot) matching the flow of passing the paper around the table.
* **Framing:**
  * Close-ups on the paper while writing/solving to show clarity of the task.
  * Medium reaction shots to capture emotional beats (confusion, fast-paced robotic writing, triumphant cheering).

---

### 4. Text Placement & Narration
* **Visual Labels:** Simple, bold text identifiers (`Student`, `Teacher`, and the logo on the face mask) are anchored to characters to eliminate the need for spoken setup.
* **No Spoken Dialogue Dependency:** The story is conveyed 100% visually through the movement of the paper and physical actions.

---

### 5. Sound & Comic Payoff
* **Sound Design:** 
  * Playful comedic background music creates an upbeat, satirical atmosphere.
  * Pop-culture sound effects (crying baby, expressive dialogue sample, cheering/celebration audio) accentuate each character's reaction.
* **Punchline / Comic Twist:** The humor stems from the student outsourcing the entire educational loop (doing homework, getting it graded) directly to the AI, claiming victory without contributing anything.

---

### 6. Abstract Reusable Formula

1. **Setup (0–3s):** Label three entities in an assembly line: **Originator** (Assigns task), **Intended Worker** (Struggling user), and **Automated Proxy** (Does the work).
2. **Action Loop (4–30s):**
   - Originator presents a basic task to Worker.
   - Worker immediately hands the task to the Proxy.
   - Proxy executes both the work and the subsequent verification steps effortlessly.
3. **Payoff / Climax (30–38s):**
   - The loop completes successfully.
   - Worker takes full credit and celebrates with high enthusiasm despite zero involvement.

## https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eN_qUYLQBqY

### Structural Analysis

#### 1. Opening Hook & Initial Dynamic
* **Visual Presentation:** An image-based meme is overlaid in the central upper third of a 9:16 vertical frame, superimposed on top of continuous first-person parkour gameplay.
* **Immediate Hook:** The viewer is engaged simultaneously by the kinetic movement of the background gameplay and a quick-consumption relatable humorous image/text combo.

#### 2. Concrete Visual Action & Layout
* **Background Layer:** Fast-paced, fluid 3D first-person platforming/jumping gameplay navigating colorful geometric courses and obstacle paths.
* **Foreground Overlay:** Centered graphic cards featuring static images, sequential panels, and superimposed caption text.
* **Aspect Ratio & Framing:** Vertical (9:16) orientation optimized for short-form feed consumption.

#### 3. Shot-Change & Transition Timing
* **Pacing:** Rapid cut cadence, averaging **1.0 to 2.5 seconds per meme**, timed directly to the narration/read speed of the caption.
* **Background Continuity:** The gameplay layer runs uninterrupted across the background while the static image cards cycle rapidly in the center.

#### 4. Narration, Sound, & Text Implementation
* **Audio Role:** A synthesized text-to-speech (TTS) voiceover reads each meme's textual setup and punchline verbatim.
* **Background Audio:** Subtle upbeat instrumental background music running underneath the spoken audio to maintain rhythm.
* **Text Placement:** Standard meme typography located directly on the central card assets (black text on white headers, or white centered text within panels).

#### 5. Comic Setup & Payoff Structure
* **Setup Phase:** Premise stated via top-line text setting up a common, relatable everyday inconvenience, social awkwardness, or internal thought.
* **Payoff Phase:** Visual reaction image, exaggerated comparison panel, or contrasting second line delivering an instantaneous comedic punchline.

---

### Abstract Reusable Structure

```
[Short-Form Content Blueprint: Rapid-Fire Relatable Meme Gallery]

1. BASE LAYER (Visual Retention Engine)
   - Continuous high-velocity gameplay / satisfying loop mechanics.
   - Fluid camera movement running without hard cuts to keep eye engagement.

2. CONTENT LAYER (Meme Slideshow)
   - Screen Position: Centered (middle third or upper center).
   - Display Duration: 1.0 – 2.5 seconds per card (matched to text length).
   - Card Format:
     * Split-panel comparison (Expectation vs. Reality / Me vs. Others)
     * Reaction image paired with a relatable situational prompt.

3. AUDIO DESIGN (Pacing & Delivery)
   - Primary: Fast-cadence TTS / neutral voiceover reading setup and punchline.
   - Secondary: Low-volume rhythmic background beat to maintain overall tempo.
```

## Original production direction

Use clearly readable roles and an immediate situation; show a concrete action or contrast; finish with the consequence or reaction that makes the setup funny. Keep original spoken copy and reviewed music/effects. Do not reuse the source actors, memes, imagery, dialogue, pop-culture sound samples, or gameplay.
